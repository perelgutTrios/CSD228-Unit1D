📱 Open in Safari

Make sure you're viewing this page in Safari browser on your iPhone or iPad. Other browsers (Chrome, Firefox, etc.) don't support PWA installation on iOS.

2

🔗 Navigate to Calculator

Go to your calculator app URL. If you're testing locally, make sure your iPhone is on the same WiFi network as your computer.

🚀 Open Calculator App
3

📤 Tap Share Button

In Safari, tap the Share button (square with arrow pointing up) in the bottom toolbar.

📱 Tap the Share icon at the bottom of Safari
4

🏠 Add to Home Screen

Scroll down in the share menu and tap "Add to Home Screen". You might need to scroll down to find this option.

🏠 Look for "Add to Home Screen" option
5

✏️ Customize Name (Optional)

You can change the app name if you want, then tap "Add" in the top right corner.

6

🎉 Launch Your App!

The Calculator app will now appear on your home screen like a native app. Tap it to launch!

✅ Perfect Setup!
