🧪 Flutter Tip Calculator Debug Instructions

Current Issue Analysis

Based on your feedback, the app loads and shows two layouts, but clicking "Calculate with this tip" doesn't update the display.

🔍 Debugging Steps

Step 1: Open your Flutter app and open Browser Developer Tools (F12)
Step 2: Go to the Console tab to see any error messages
Step 3: Enter bill amount ($50), tax ($5), and number of guests (2)
Step 4: Look at the tip recommendations that appear to the right
Step 5: Click "Calculate with this tip" on any recommendation
Step 6: Check the console for any error messages when you click the button
Step 7: Look for a "Payment Breakdown" section that should appear below the recommendations

📱 Expected Behavior

When you click "Calculate with this tip", a new section called "📋 Payment Breakdown" should appear below the tip recommendations showing:

  • Original total
  • Adjusted total (with smart rounding)
  • Amount per person
  • Final tip amount
  • Actual tip percentage

🚨 What to Look For

  • Console Errors: Any red error messages in the browser console
  • Button Response: Does the button text change from "Calculate with this tip" to "✅ Selected"?
  • New Section: Does a "Payment Breakdown" section appear below the recommendations?
  • Scroll Down: The breakdown might appear below - try scrolling down after clicking
